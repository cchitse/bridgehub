# BridgeHub

BridgeHub gives users access to supported open data sources through their preferred AI applications.

## Language

**Supported source**:
An open data provider whose data BridgeHub makes available. The initial provider is the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), through EDGAR.
_Avoid_: Tool, AI application

**Company search**:
A lookup of SEC filers by ticker, company name, or CIK, returning matching company profiles as described in SEC.md.
_Avoid_: Financial statement analysis

**Company profile**:
A set of descriptive attributes for an SEC filer, including its identifiers, name, industry classification, exchange, fiscal year end, incorporation state, and recent filings count.

**Source provenance**:
The official source URLs, retrieval time, and query associated with a returned result, allowing a user to trace it to its originating data source.
_Avoid_: AI-generated justification
